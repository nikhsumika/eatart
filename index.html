=EAT(ART)

A new way to consume art.

by Gregory Nikshumika

A color puzzle hidden inside a spreadsheet. Every cell is a pixel of a famous painting: mix red, green and blue to match it, and consume the picture one bite at a time.

Seasons of masterpieces, a new painting every day, and Blank Sheet, where every painting starts empty and you paint it back.

Coming soon to the App Store. The iPhone app is in testing now. Want to try it? Write to gregorynikshumika@gmail.com.
