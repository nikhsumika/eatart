=EAT(ART) Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

=EAT(ART) is a game by Gregory Nikshumika. This policy covers the iOS app and the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, stars, streaks and settings are saved on your device only, so you can pick up where you left off. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

If you use My photo, the picture you choose is turned into a game sheet on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. Share cards are saved to Photos only when you choose to save them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your Apple ID owns the unlock.

Sharing

When you share a result, the text and image go wherever you send them through the iOS share sheet. The game does not keep a copy.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
